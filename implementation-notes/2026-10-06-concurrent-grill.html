Concurrent grill

branch: none · worktree: jevburger · 2026-10-06 · ready-for-review · blast: medium

Summary

GoalJev cooks two tickets simultaneously and controls when each patty goes on or off the grill.
SurfaceKitchen wrapper, Jev decision options, spatula and slot art.
SchemaTwo burger states, slot-targeted actions, one shared cooking clock.
MobileTwo visible grill positions and compact ticket labels.

Algorithm

  1. Offer legal moves for each active ticket tagged with its slot plus one shared wait action.
  2. One wait cooks every patty on the grill for 30 simulated seconds.
  3. Jev chooses place, flip, remove and when to advance time; revalidate against both slots.

UI layout

  1. Show two patties in the grill and two plates/tickets.
  2. Animate spatula to the affected slot for place, flip and remove.
  3. Auto-fill an empty position when a parsed ticket arrives.

Reversibility

Kitchen wrapper and art can revert without modifying the single-burger transition rules.

Definition of done

  • Two tickets progress independently while one wait cooks both.
  • Spatula moves on placement, flipping and removal.
  • Jev serves two overlapping tickets; tests/build/mobile check pass.
