Decisions

IDChoiceRationale
D-001Use Three.js with authored procedural meshes and an orthographic fixed camera.No modeling CLI is available; real mesh geometry and lighting meet the 3D request without a brittle external asset pipeline.
D-002Keep SVG as WebGL failure fallback; keep ticket UI in HTML.Accessible ordering and non-WebGL support should not depend on GPU availability.
D-003Render two framed views on mobile rather than squish a single scene.One camera crop cannot keep both the grill and four plates legible at 320px.
D-004Lazy-load the 3D module while rendering ticket controls immediately.Keep the ordering UI responsive before the renderer bundle arrives.
D-005Animate geometry only for the current kitchen action; render idle frames on demand.Motion explains contact and stacking without an always-running render loop.

Tradeoffs

IDPicked over rejectedReason
T-001Procedural meshes over external glTF assets.Keep models versioned, deterministically state-driven and shippable without Blender.
T-002Two mobile canvases over unreadably small food in one view.Cap device pixel ratio to 1.5; use 1024/512 shadow maps for grill/plates.
T-003Lazy 587KB Three.js chunk over static SVG-only rendering.True lit 3D geometry requires a GPU renderer; main ticket chunk remains 241KB.

Reversibility

Single UI scene swap; revert the commit to restore SVG without touching cooking data.
